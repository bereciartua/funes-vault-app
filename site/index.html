Give AI agents your memory. Not all of it.

Funes Vault keeps what agents should know about you on your Mac, and lets each one see just the bits you allow. It sees everything; it says very little.

Same vault. Different agents.

Your memories live in one vault. Each agent gets its own key and its own window into it: what it may read, up to which sensitivity, in which categories. Pick an agent and see what it can see.

Agents suggest. You stamp.

When an agent learns something worth keeping, it can suggest a memory or a correction. By default it waits in your Inbox, with who proposed it and why, until you apply it, reject it, or leave it for later.

Your own captures land there too: press ⌥⌘M anywhere, or select text and choose Remember in Funes. Import your ChatGPT memories or Claude’s to start; anything that looks like a secret stays out.

Agents in the cloud

claude.ai, ChatGPT and cloud coding agents reach Funes through a tunnel you run, such as Tailscale Funnel. One click in the menu bar cuts them all off.

  • claude.ai
  • ChatGPT
  • Gemini Enterprise
  • cloud coding agents

Remote Access walks you through it: choose a tunnel, turn it on, paste its address, test. Funes never starts a tunnel; it shows you the command.

claude.ai and ChatGPT sign in with a six-digit code you compare on your Mac. While Remote Access is on, the vault is reachable at a public address, and what you share with a cloud agent goes to its vendor.

Things Funes doesn’t have

Your vault lives in ~/Library/Application Support/FunesVault, and that’s the only place it lives.

  • An account
  • A server
  • A cloud copy
  • Analytics
  • Telemetry
  • A crash reporter
  • Cookies on this site

What does leave: what you let agents have, a daily update check to GitHub, and, only if you add an AI provider, capture text at or below your cloud limit, never anything that looks like a secret. With Remote Access on, also a test request through your tunnel and, while pairing, the signing-in app’s public metadata.

The honest limits: other programs running as you can read the vault, and Funes can’t take text back once an agent has it.

Funes Vault is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0. Releases are notarized by Apple, updates are signed, and installing one always takes your click. It’s named after Borges’ “Funes the Memorious”, a man who could forget nothing. It’s an independent project, not affiliated with Apple, Anthropic or OpenAI.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Open source under the Apache License 2.0, with no account or trial.

Does it always ask before sharing?

By default, yes. You can turn Ask before sharing off for an agent you trust; it then gets what it may read without a panel, and each share still shows in Activity.

What if I’m away when an agent asks?

The agent waits 45 seconds, then is told the request is waiting. It stays in Pending requests in the menu bar for 15 minutes; share it then and the agent gets it the next time it checks.

Can an agent change or delete my memories?

No. Agents can only propose new memories and corrections. One you give Write, with Ask before saving off, can save new memories directly within its limits; its corrections still wait in your Inbox.

Does it need Apple Intelligence?

No. Search uses a small model built into the app. On macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence, Apple’s on-device model proposes titles and categories for captures; otherwise simple rules do.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. An iPhone companion that captures thoughts and searches your vault is planned.
