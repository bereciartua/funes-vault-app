Support

Funes Vault is a free public preview, supported on a best-effort basis by its maintainer.

Report a problem or ask for a feature

Open an issue on GitHub Issues. It helps to include:

  • your Funes Vault version (Funes Vault ▸ About Funes Vault, with a Funes Vault window open) and macOS version;
  • the agent you connected (for example Claude Code or Cursor) and its version;
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • for connection problems, the output of /Applications/FunesVault.app/Contents/MacOS/funes-mcp check --agent <agent ID> (it prints no keys).

Issues are public. Never paste memories, agent keys or remote tokens (anything starting with fns_, such as fns_mcp_v1_… or fns_mcpr_v1_…), or AI provider API keys, into an issue or a screenshot.

Security

Please report vulnerabilities privately, not in a public issue: use GitHub’s private vulnerability reporting; only the maintainer sees it. SECURITY.md describes what’s in scope, the known limits, and how reports are handled.

Common questions

An agent can’t see Funes Vault

Make sure Funes Vault is running (its icon is in the menu bar) and the agent has access on its page in the app. Then run the funes-mcp check command above, and see Connect agents for each agent’s setup. After changing an agent’s MCP settings, restart the agent.

Where is my data?

Only on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/FunesVault. Back it up with the rest of your Mac (Time Machine), or export the vault from Settings ▸ Data. Exports never contain keys or access settings.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. With Apple Intelligence (macOS 26 or later), Funes Vault uses Apple’s on-device model to propose titles and categories for new captures. Without it, simple rules on your Mac propose them. Search and agent access work the same either way.

Does anything leave my Mac?

Only what you allow agents to have, a daily update check to GitHub, and, if you add one, what you send to an AI provider of your choice. With Remote Access on (it’s off by default), cloud agents you allow get what their access permits through the tunnel you run, and Funes tests your tunnel and, while pairing, fetches the signing-in app’s public metadata. The privacy statement has the details.

How do I uninstall it?

If you used Remote Access, turn your tunnel off first; the Remote Access page shows how. Then remove Funes Vault from your agents’ MCP settings, quit the app from its menu, and move FunesVault.app to the Trash. Your vault stays in ~/Library/Application Support/FunesVault until you delete that folder; the app’s settings are in ~/Library/Preferences/com.funesvault.mac.plist, and if you linked the launcher, delete ~/.local/bin/funes-mcp. If you installed with Homebrew, brew uninstall --zap --cask funes-vault removes the app and moves the data folder, caches and preferences to the Trash. API keys for AI providers you added are in your Keychain under com.funesvault.provider.

Funes Vault for iPhone

The iPhone app is a companion to Funes Vault on your Mac: your vault lives on the Mac, and the iPhone captures, searches, edits, reviews the Inbox and answers agents’ requests there. It needs Funes Vault on a Mac (macOS 15 or later). Without one, Try Demo on the Capture tab shows every screen with sample data.

How do I pair my iPhone?

On the Mac, open Funes Vault Settings ▸ iPhone, turn on Allow iPhone access and click Pair iPhone…. On the iPhone, tap Pair with Mac and scan the code, then click Allow on the Mac. The two need to reach each other: the same Wi-Fi, Tailscale on both, or a tunnel you set up for Remote Access.

The iPhone says “Mac not reachable”

Tap Why? on the banner: it lists what it tried and what to check. Usually the Mac is asleep, Funes Vault isn’t running, the two aren’t on the same Wi-Fi, or Local Network access is off for Funes in the iPhone’s Settings ▸ Privacy & Security. Captures wait on the iPhone and go to the Mac by themselves once it’s back.

Approval notifications don’t arrive

They need notifications on for Funes, approvals on in the iPhone’s Settings ▸ Approvals and for that iPhone on the Mac, and the same Apple Account with iCloud on both devices. The iPhone’s Settings ▸ Approvals says which is missing. Requests still show under Waiting for you on the Capture tab, and on the Mac.

What does the iPhone keep?

No copy of your vault: only captures waiting for your Mac, and the pairing keys in its Keychain. Unpair This iPhone… in Settings removes them; removing the iPhone on the Mac in Settings ▸ iPhone cuts it off at once, for a lost phone. The privacy statement has the details.

Reporting an iPhone problem

Open an issue as above, with your iOS version and iPhone model, the Funes Vault version on both devices (the iPhone’s is in the App Store or TestFlight), and how the two connect (Wi-Fi, Tailscale or a tunnel).
