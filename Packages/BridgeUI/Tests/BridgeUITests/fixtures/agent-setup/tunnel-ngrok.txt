ngrok
name: ngrok
summary: A free dev domain with an ngrok account. TLS ends at ngrok.
preservesHost: false
detectionHeader: X-Forwarded-Host
installLink: https://ngrok.com/download

commands:
ngrok config add-authtoken <your ngrok authtoken>
ngrok http 47627 --url https://calm-otter-42.ngrok-free.app --host-header=rewrite

run: ngrok http 47627 --url https://calm-otter-42.ngrok-free.app --host-header=rewrite

steps:
1. Sign up at ngrok.com, then put your authtoken and your free dev domain from the dashboard into the commands.
2. Paste https:// and your dev domain in step 3.
3. To turn it off, press Control-C in the Terminal window running ngrok.

notes:
- TLS ends at ngrok's edge, so ngrok can read the traffic, memories included.
- The free plan allows 20,000 requests a month.
- Don't turn on ngrok's basic auth: it takes over the Authorization header that carries the token.
- Optionally, allow only your agent's addresses with a Traffic Policy IP restriction, for example Anthropic's 160.79.104.0/21.
