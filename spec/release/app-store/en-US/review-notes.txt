Funes Vault for iPhone is the companion of Funes Vault for Mac, a free, open-source app (https://funesvault.com) that keeps a private memory for the user's AI agents on their Mac. The iPhone app has no account and no server: it pairs with the user's own Mac by scanning a QR code the Mac shows, and then talks only to that Mac, end to end encrypted, over the local network, Tailscale or the user's tunnel. Without a paired Mac, Search and the Inbox have nothing to show.

DEMO MODE (no Mac needed)
1. Launch the app. It opens on the Capture tab.
2. Under "Pair with your Mac to start", tap "Try Demo". (Also in Settings: "Try the Demo"; and on the Search and Inbox tabs.)
3. An orange "DEMO" banner, "Demo vault: not your data", stays at the top. The demo uses a sample vault built into the app; nothing in it is sent anywhere.

Things to try in the demo:
- Capture: "Waiting for you" lists three agent requests. Open "Claude Code wants 4 memories" to see the Share panel: untick a memory and tap Share. The other two are a Save request (Save / Discard / Later) and an access request (Allow Once / Not Now).
- Capture: type something and tap Remember. It's sent to the demo Inbox.
- Search: search "garden", open a memory, tap Edit, change the text and Save. Archive, Restore and Delete are on the same screen; Archived and Recently Deleted are scopes above the results.
- Inbox: swipe right to Save a suggestion, left to Reject, or tap one for Save, Edit & Save… and Reject.
- Settings: the Demo section; "Leave Demo" in the banner or the Demo Vault switch returns to the unpaired app and discards the demo's changes.

WHAT NEEDS A REAL MAC
- Pairing (Settings ▸ Pair with Mac, which opens the camera to scan the Mac's code).
- Approval notifications: when an agent's request waits on the Mac, the Mac saves a record holding only a random tag in the user's own private iCloud database (CloudKit), whose push wakes the iPhone; the notification service extension then fetches the request from the Mac and words the alert. Demo mode shows the same panels from the Capture tab's "Waiting for you" list.

PERMISSIONS, each asked only when used
- Local Network: to find and reach the user's Mac on their Wi-Fi.
- Camera: to scan the pairing code.
- Microphone and Speech Recognition: dictation on the Capture tab, transcribed on the iPhone.
- Notifications: asked once paired with approvals on, for agents' requests.
- Background App Refresh (fetch): to send captures made from the share sheet, Shortcuts or the Control while the app was closed, once the Mac is reachable.

PRIVACY
Data Not Collected. No analytics, tracking or third-party code. Privacy policy: https://funesvault.com/privacy/ — Support: https://funesvault.com/support/

EXPORT COMPLIANCE
The app encrypts its messages to the user's own Mac with Apple's CryptoKit (HPKE, AES-GCM, P-256 signatures) and uses HTTPS through URLSession for a tunnel. No other encryption.
