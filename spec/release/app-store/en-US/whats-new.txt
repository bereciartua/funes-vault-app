This update fixes what a full review of the app turned up, mostly security and reliability.

SAFER
• Your iPhone now acts only on replies your Mac has signed, so another device on the same Wi-Fi can't crash the app, move its clock or make it say it was removed.
• Stopping dictation while it's starting no longer leaves the microphone on.

FIXED
• A search answer quoting part of an emoji no longer crashes the app.
• Home's counts and the widgets are no longer cleared at launch or in a background refresh.
• If your Mac can't check this iPhone for a moment, the app says your vault isn't available, not that the iPhone was removed.
• Pairing asks for camera access the first time, and Cancel while waiting for Allow on your Mac cancels the pairing there too.
• After unpairing, a late reply no longer shows Connected or brings the counts back.
• An answered Inbox card no longer comes back, and a request that arrives while the list is loading now shows.
• A reload that fails after "Changed on your Mac" keeps your edit.
• Captures with unusual characters are saved whole, and sharing a very large text is refused instead of failing.

Update Funes Vault for Mac to 0.5.2 first: with an older Mac, this version shows your Mac as not reachable.
