Facts: Funes can now learn, from your memories on your Mac, who the people in them are to you and where you've worked, so your searches and answers know them too. It's off by default and turned on in Funes Vault for Mac (Settings ▸ AI ▸ Features ▸ Facts).

NEW
• People & History, from Search's toolbar: the people, organizations and places your memories mention, what each is to you and how Funes knows, the memories that mention them, your work timeline, and what Funes couldn't settle. Read live from your Mac; nothing is kept on the iPhone.
• Search results found through a fact say why, for example "My sister → Maria".
• Answers mark claims that rest on something Funes inferred and, with a cloud model on your Mac, say "not sure" when they can't check a claim about your work history.
• Correct Funes from your phone: Not Maria when a memory isn't about her, Not This when a fact is wrong.

Update Funes Vault for Mac to 0.6.0 first: with 0.5.2 on your Mac, the app works as before, without People & History.
