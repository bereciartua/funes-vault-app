# ADR-0030: Settings gets a sidebar; AI splits into features, accounts and limits; an account offers several models

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-30 (after two GPT-6-Astra reviews of the draft against this repository) · **Amends** [FR-AI-05](../../product/03-requirements.md) (models per task), [FR-AI-06](../../product/03-requirements.md) (provider credentials), [FR-AI-12](../../product/03-requirements.md) (usage counters), FR-AI-16 (subscriptions), FR-AI-17 (its Settings path), [ADR-0014](0014-user-selectable-models.md), [ADR-0024](0024-subscription-providers.md) (one model per plan), [model providers §2 and §4](../model-providers.md), [UX: Mac ▸ Surfaces and Settings](../../product/04-ux-mac.md#settings), and every "Settings ▸ AI Models" and "Settings ▸ MCP" reference in the spec, docs and both apps.

## Context
- **The window has outgrown its tabs.** Settings is a toolbar `TabView` with nine tabs (About You · General · Capture · AI Models · MCP · iPhone · Data · Updates · Advanced) in a 700×580 window: the labels run edge to edge and can't be grouped. About 80 controls in all.
- **AI Models is the hard part.** Its 31 rows switch 26 times between five kinds of thing: accounts (where a model comes from), routing (which model does a job), feature behaviour (split, schedule, when to answer), rules (cloud limit, daily cap, auto-apply), and live status, plus four disclosure lines and long captions. The **New memories** picker is about 20 rows above the accounts that fill it; on day one it offers only Apple on-device, and the place to add Claude or a key is at the bottom, under two headings ("Use your subscription", "Providers") that look unrelated.
- **The author's feedback (30 Oct 2026):** "the AI model section has gotten very, very confusing because it's mixing available models, connected accounts, and how those models are being used." Hard for a first-time user, even if not for the author.
- **The code fuses account and model.** FR-AI-05 and model providers §4 say each task has "a provider and a model", but `ProviderConfig` holds exactly one chat `model`, and each plan (`SubscriptionConfig`) exactly one too. A task picks an account and gets whatever model that account holds. So Haiku for New memories and Opus for the tidy-up on one Anthropic key or one Claude plan isn't possible, and there's no workaround: the Add menu disables a preset that's already there, and the custom path creates only OpenAI-compatible endpoints. Embeddings, meanwhile, already store provider and model separately (`EmbeddingChoice`).
- **Five proposals were compared** with mockups (P1 Regroup, P2 Simple + Advanced, P3 Settings in context, P4 AI as a place, P5 Privacy first), and GPT-6-Astra reviewed them against this repository. **The author chose P1**, with the account/model split, and left the extras (a simple "How should Funes use AI?" question, a Set Up AI assistant, a privacy Overview, shortcuts from features) for later.

## Decision
1. **A sidebar window.** Settings becomes a sidebar window like System Settings: a `NavigationSplitView` in today's AppKit-hosted window, with the sidebar toggle removed and a fixed sidebar width. A short prototype checks this first (Phase 11.0) on the macOS versions the app supports; if the sidebar can't be kept from collapsing or resizing, an `HStack` with a `List` and a divider is the fallback, with the same look. Default size 820×600, minimum 760×520 (the frame autosave stays). Panes stay grouped `Form`s. Some rows open a **page** inside the pane (a job, an account).
   - **Phase 11.0 found the fallback is needed** (macOS 27.0.1, the only version on hand): in the AppKit-hosted window, `NavigationSplitView` ignored `navigationSplitViewColumnWidth` (fixed or min = ideal = max = 210; the sidebar came up 144 pt wide, as the system's floating sidebar), its divider could still be dragged (to 320 pt), and the sidebar could still collapse (dragging the divider to the edge, or ⌃⌘S's `toggleSidebar:`), with `.toolbar(removing: .sidebarToggle)` and a constant `.all` column visibility. So Settings is an `HStack` of a 210 pt sidebar `List` (`.sidebar` style), a divider, and the pane's `NavigationStack`, in a window without a full-size content view. The minimum size held either way. The fallback doesn't depend on the system's split view, so it behaves the same on macOS 15.
   - **Titles:** the window's title is the pane's name; on a page, the toolbar shows a back button named after the pane ("‹ Features") and the page's title.
   - **Back** works within a pane only (the button, ⌘[); it never crosses panes. Choosing a pane in the sidebar shows its top level.
   - **Opening:** ⌘, and every Settings… open the last pane shown, at its top level, or General the first time. A deep link opens its page.
2. **Eleven panes in four groups** (UX in [UX: Mac ▸ Settings](../../product/04-ux-mac.md#settings)):
   - a **profile row** at the top for **About You** (initials and name, or "About You" while the profile is empty);
   - **General** (appearance, open at login, Dock, and Updates, folded in), **Notifications** (moved out of General), **Capture & Shortcuts** (both global shortcuts, capture sensitivity, Services);
   - **AI:** **Features**, **Models & Accounts**, **Privacy & Limits**;
   - **Connections:** **Agent Server** (today's MCP tab, renamed; "MCP" stays in its caption), **iPhone**;
   - **Vault:** **Data & Backups** (today's Data), **Advanced**.
3. **AI ▸ Features: one row per job.** New memories, Daily tidy-up, Search answers and Search index, each summarized in one line (what it does, which model, a locality badge, or what's missing) and opening its own page with that job's options, status and disclosure line. Apply changes automatically stays on the pane, under the jobs. The task names don't change.
4. **AI ▸ Models & Accounts: where models come from.** Grouped by how they're paid for: **Built in** (Apple on-device, bge-small), **Your plans** (Claude through Claude Code, ChatGPT through Codex, always listed with their state), **API keys** and **Local servers** (by the account's resolved locality; cloud until resolved). Each row says which jobs use it. A row opens the **account page**: its connection fields, its **models**, Used by, Test Connection, usage, and Remove. **Add Account…** has today's presets and Other OpenAI-Compatible Server….
5. **AI ▸ Privacy & Limits: what may leave.** The cloud limit (with its one-time confirmation above Internal), cloud requests per day with today's count, and a **summary** of what the AI features send, built from the same facts as the disclosure lines. A job is listed whenever it can send anything: New memories while proposals *or* splitting is on (splitting uses the model even with proposals off); the tidy-up whenever it has a model, scheduled or not (Tidy Up Now works with the schedule off); Search answers while Answer isn't Off, with the effective trigger (Apple on-device only when asked); the second index with what it sends, the agent-request switch and the cloud limit Nothing case included. It covers AI only (agents, Remote Access and the iPhone are out of scope here, see 11).
6. **An account offers several models.** Stored settings change shape:
   - An API or local-server account keeps its endpoint and name and gets **`models`**, an ordered list of chat models (replacing `model`). A plan gets the same list (replacing its single `model`); a new plan's list starts with its suggestions (Claude: `haiku`, `opus`; ChatGPT: `gpt-6-luna`, `gpt-5.6-terra`). Apple on-device has one implicit model. Embedding models aren't in the list: the second index keeps its own `EmbeddingChoice`.
   - **A job's choice is one of three kinds:** *follows New memories* (Daily tidy-up and Search answers only, their default), *Apple on-device* (New memories' default; Search answers can choose it; never the tidy-up), or an **account and model** (`ModelChoice`). A `ModelChoice` can be **unresolved**: an account chosen with no model (only the conversion makes one). An unresolved choice never borrows another job's model: the job shows "Choose a model" and runs as today's provider without a model does (New memories: on-device, then rules; the tidy-up: its code-only rules; Search answers: unavailable).
   - **Pickers** list "Apple on-device" where allowed, then every model of every configured account, grouped by account ("Claude (Claude Code)": haiku, opus), then **Add a Model…**, which opens Models & Accounts. An account that can't run now stays listed with why ("No key", "Not signed in", "Needs your OK to read files"), so a current choice is always visible; a plan whose CLI isn't found shows only while chosen (today's rule). An account with no models shows "No models" (disabled).
   - **Job status comes from configuration only:** unresolved, the account's key missing, a plan's state from `refreshSubscriptions`, a CLI blocked after a tool event (per account, as today), Apple Intelligence unavailable. Test Connection's result stays on the account page, says which model it tested, and doesn't mark the others. No stored per-model health.
   - **Removing a chat model** that jobs choose directly asks first, naming them, and resets them to their default (New memories to Apple on-device; the others to follows New memories). Jobs that follow New memories keep following, and the question names them too ("Search answers follows New memories and will use Apple on-device"). Embeddings are never affected by a chat model's removal.
   - **Removing an account** asks first, naming every job that uses it, the second index included. As today, it deletes the key (FR-AI-06), clears its usage, resets its jobs, and clears the second index if it's this account's, which stops indexing and deletes its vectors.
   - **Unchanged:** keys in the Keychain by account id; usage counters and CLI blocking by account, not model; locality by endpoint; calibrations by `<account>/<model>`; the tidy-up's auto-apply levels, measured on the chosen model's id.
   - **What re-embeds:** as today, a change to the embedding choice, the cloud limit (lowering it deletes the vectors of memories now above it, FR-AI-11) or a calibration. Among account edits, only the second index's account address, key or cloud flag re-embeds; renaming an account or editing its chat models doesn't (today's `upsert` restarts the index on any edit to that account).
   - **The answering engine's name includes its model** ("Claude (Claude Code) · opus"), in Settings and in `status.get`, so the iPhone, which clears its answer card and kept answer when the engine's name or locality changes, also does when only the model changes, directly or through New memories.
   - **One conversion of the author's settings** at first launch: each provider's `model` becomes its `models` (empty stays empty; an embedding model saved as the chat model, which v0.7 allowed with a warning and which never chatted, is left out, as in the author's own settings, so a task on that provider is unresolved); each plan's `models` is its stored model (if not blank) followed by its suggestions, or just the suggestions without a record; each task's account id becomes that account with its model, or an unresolved choice when the account had none or the plan's model was blank; "same as New memories" (nil) and `apple.foundation` stay as they are. The old fields are dropped when the new shape is written. No compatibility beyond that (no outside users).
   - **Where the code goes:** the stored shape, the conversion and choice resolution move into **FunesCore** (`Models/`) with unit tests, holding no localized text. Names, states and disclosure sentences stay in the app, built from facts the core returns. `ModelSettings` stays the app's observable wrapper.
7. **Status you act on stays beside its control.** A missing key, a plan that isn't signed in, embedding progress with Cancel or Retry, a tidy-up that failed, today's cloud requests, a server that didn't start. Counters are history, and they move one level down rather than to Activity: usage to each account's and plan's page (with **Reset Usage** for that account, instead of one button for all), the agent server's traffic counts to the bottom of its pane, where they already are. Apple on-device's counts aren't shown, as today.
8. **Disclosure stays at every choice.** Every job page keeps its disclosure line under its model choice, worded as today (FR-AI-08), and the Features rows and the Privacy & Limits summary are additions, never replacements. The capture disclosure stays in Capture & Shortcuts.
9. **Deep links name a destination**, not a tab: a pane, or a page inside one (`ai.features/tidy-up`, `ai.accounts/<account id>`). Every "AI Models…" button and "Settings ▸ AI Models" sentence points to the specific place. When a job fails because of its account (a missing key, not signed in, Codex not allowed, a blocked CLI), the link goes to the account's page; otherwise to the job's page. A destination for an account that no longer exists opens Models & Accounts. `FUNES_DEBUG_OPEN=settings:<destination>` uses the same names; snapshots use a filesystem-safe form (`settings-ai-features-tidy-up`).
10. **Nothing else changes.** Every setting keeps its meaning, default, storage key and side effects (login item, Dock policy, notification permission, re-embedding, the server restart on a port change, the tunnel path reset), apart from 6. No option is removed; [Phase 11's control map](../../plans/phase-11-settings.md#control-map) lists where each control goes.
11. **Out of scope** (possible later, each on its own): a simple mode or Advanced switch, a Set Up AI assistant, a Settings Overview of everything that leaves the Mac, a search field in Settings, settings moved into the main window (search answers in the Vault, tidy-up schedule in the Inbox, iPhone in the sidebar), and an AI section in the main window.

## Consequences
- **Easier to explain, same power.** A first-time reader meets AI as three questions in three panes. The author loses nothing, and gains a different model per job on one account.
- **More panes, each shorter.** Eleven panes instead of nine tabs; the longest becomes Features' Search index page, which is today's Embeddings rows.
- **The settings shape changes once.** The conversion runs on the author's Mac at first launch of the new build, with tests for every v1 shape seen in the code (providers with and without a model, plans with a model, a blank model or no record, the follow-New-memories nil, `apple.foundation`, a task pointing at a model-less account).
- **Many strings move.** "Settings ▸ AI Models" appears in 18 user-facing app strings (3 of them on the iPhone), the MCP launcher's message names "Settings ▸ MCP", and the spec, docs, README and PRIVACY.md mention both about 60 times. The string catalog gets the new strings and drops the old.
- **Snapshots are renamed and added:** one per pane and per page, light and dark, plus problem states (a missing key, a blocked plan, an account with no models, an unresolved choice, an embedding error).
- **No test target for the app.** The data change goes to FunesCore for tests; the window is checked by the UI-review snapshots and by hand.
- **The iPhone app changes only words**: its three strings that send people to "Settings ▸ AI Models" on the Mac.
- **The menu bar panel** (ADR-0029 and Phase 10, planned in parallel on their own branch) names Settings ▸ General for the Review Request shortcut, Settings ▸ Connections for the agent server and Settings ▸ AI Models ▸ Search answers. Whichever lands second updates them: to Capture & Shortcuts, `agent-server` and Features ▸ Search answers.
