# Phase 7: Home, widgets and the iPhone's status bar

**Goal:** bring back the web version's Home on both apps, extended with what only Funes knows: a place to add a memory right there, and the vault's pulse (what came in, what went out to your agents, its shape and rhythm), plus the same cards as iPhone widgets. On the way, the iPhone's connection banner shrinks to a bar that appears only when something needs you.

**Duration:** about 5–6 weekends. **Depends on:** nothing unfinished (7.1 can start at once). **Decision:** [ADR-0025](../architecture/decisions/0025-vault-overview-and-iphone-cache.md). **Specs:** [UX: Mac ▸ Home](../product/04-ux-mac.md#home), [UX: iPhone ▸ Home, Status bar, Widgets](../product/05-ux-iphone.md), [companion protocol `overview.get`](../architecture/companion-protocol.md#4-methods), requirements FR-H-01..06 and FR-iOS-06.

**Order:** 7.1 → 7.2 → 7.3 and 7.4 (either order) → 7.5. Each step ships on its own; the Mac and iPhone apps are updated together.

## 7.1 The iPhone's status bar · ½ weekend
Replaces the banner at the top of Capture, Search and the Inbox ([UX: iPhone ▸ Status bar](../product/05-ux-iphone.md#status-bar)).
- [x] **The bar** above the tab bar, one for the whole app: iOS 26's `tabViewBottomAccessory` where it can be hidden when there's nothing to say (check on the device whether 26.0 can; otherwise, and on iOS 18–25, a bar of our own just above the tab bar). Nothing while connected.
  - *Found (iOS 27 SDK, 2026-10-25):* 26.0 has only `tabViewBottomAccessory(content:)`, with no way to turn it off once set; 26.1 added `tabViewBottomAccessory(isEnabled:content:)`. So the accessory from 26.1, and on iOS 18–26.0 a bar of our own as a bottom `safeAreaInset` of each tab, which leaves the large titles and the search field alone. The accessory's content needs a view of its own that reads the state itself: built inline in the tab view's body, it kept its first text ("Mac not reachable" without "· 2 waiting"). Debug builds take `-FunesOwnStatusBar YES` to try ours on a newer iOS, and `-FunesConnection <state>` (connected, unreachable, localNetworkOff, failed, revoked) to show a state without a Mac. Checked in the iOS 27 simulator: connected, unreachable, not paired and the demo, with the accessory and with our own bar, and the status sheet from the bar and from Why?. In the simulator, tapping the middle of the bar (its words) often didn't open the sheet while the icon, the left edge and the button did; on an iPhone 13 mini (iOS 27.0.1) every tap works, so it was the simulator's (2026-10-25).
- [x] **States** from FunesPhone's `BannerState` (unchanged): Connecting (only after 2 s), Mac not reachable · N waiting [Why?], Local Network access is off [Fix], Can't connect to your Mac [Why?], Pair with your Mac to start [Pair], Removed from Funes on your Mac [Pair Again], Back online · N captures sent (3 s), Demo vault: not your data [Leave Demo] (always). A 2-second debounce before showing any state.
- [x] **Tapping the bar** opens the status sheet (today's Why sheet: which Mac, last reached, the queue with Send Now, the hints, Try Again); the bar's button acts directly.
- [x] **Remove** `BannerSection` from Capture, Search and the Inbox; their full-page explanations (`VaultUnavailableView`) stay. Try Demo moves to Capture's (later Home's) pairing card.
- [x] VoiceOver (one element, the action as a custom action, one announcement per change), Reduce Motion (fade), Dynamic Type; string catalog.
- [x] Tests in FunesPhone for the debounce and the "Back online" transition; the screenshot scenes and the App Store screenshots plan updated (the DEMO label moves to the bar).
- [x] When the code lands: README, docs, CHANGELOG.

- [x] **Benchmark** on a 10,000-memory fixture vault: under 50 ms, or add the two indexes in a migration (with its fixture test). *(`OverviewBenchmarkTests`: 10,000 memories, 8,000 delivered requests with about 24,000 items, 2,500 others, each with a 20-memory candidate snapshot. ## 7.4 iPhone Home · 1 weekend
[UX: iPhone ▸ Home](../product/05-ux-iphone.md#home-tab).
- [x] **Home** replaces the Capture tab (where the app opens; the Control and "Remember in Funes" open it). Everything Capture had stays: the composer, Waiting for you, Recent captures. *(`HomeView`, the old `CaptureView` with the charts added; the composer's behaviour is unchanged, its field a little shorter, 96 pt, so the charts start on the first screen.)*
- [x] Order: title ("Good afternoon"), composer, Waiting for you, Pulse card (7 / 30 days, finger scrubbing with a light haptic per day), Memories · Sensitivity · Rhythm tiles, Who reads your vault, Recent captures (five, then Show All).
  - *Built:* 7 days is the last week of the 30-day overview, so the switch never asks the Mac; it starts on 30 days (not remembered: that would be the app's first own `UserDefaults`, a new required reason in the privacy manifest). The Out stack names the top three agents (by memories shared in the range), then Other; colours follow the agent's rank in the overview, so 7 and 30 days paint an agent alike. Scrubbing is **press and hold, then drag** (0.15 s): an immediate drag on a chart inside the list either stole the list's scrolling or, with Swift Charts' `chartXSelection`, never fired in the iOS 27 simulator. The numbers (FunesPhone's `Pulse`, `Growth`, `RhythmGrid`, `SensitivityMix`, `OverviewDigestTests`) are worked out on the phone from `VaultOverview`; the growth line counts back from today's total, so deletions make earlier weeks read a little high.
- [x] Taps: tiles to Search (newest, or filtered), Rhythm and the Pulse card to a sheet with the longer ranges. *(Memories: Search with no query, which lists the most recently updated. Sensitivity: a menu of the vault's levels with their counts, each opening Search filtered to it; Search's new filter button changes or clears it (`SearchModel.sensitivity`). The Pulse card's sentence row and the Rhythm tile open the sheet: 7 days · 30 days · 12 weeks · 12 months, each range asked for once shown and cached, and the 12-week Rhythm grid with the busiest weekday and the streak.)*
- [x] **States:** live when connected; the cache dimmed with "As of 10:42" while the Mac is away; a Pair with your Mac card (with Try Demo) in place of the charts when not paired; demo data in the demo; a new vault's empty states. *(Dimmed whenever the Mac isn't connected, whatever the snapshot's age; "As of 21 Oct, 10:42" once it's not today's. `-FunesConnection unreachable` shows the demo's counts as if cached at 10:42 when there's no cache. A range the cache doesn't have says "This range shows once your Mac can be reached".)*
- [x] Shared chart views in `Apps/FunesShared`, so the widgets draw the same thing. *(`OverviewCharts.swift`: `PulseChart` (with an optional selection: the widgets pass none), `PulseLegend`, `GrowthLine`, `SensitivityBars`, `RhythmGridView`, the colours and the words; SwiftUI and Swift Charts only. Compiled into the app and the Intents extension, which builds with it.)*
- [x] VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion; string catalogs; screenshot scene `home` (and the App Store set's first shot). *(The chart is one element, "Last 30 days: 83 in · 194 shared · 2 refused", with an audio graph (In and Shared) and swipe up or down to step through the days, each read as its breakdown; each tile is one button; the tiles stack from xxLarge text; the range switch doesn't animate with Reduce Motion. The colours were checked with the dataviz palette validator: neighbours in each stack stay apart for colour blindness, light and dark, and the legend names every series.)*
- [x] When the code lands: PRIVACY.md (the overview cache), the App Store privacy notes, README, docs, CHANGELOG. *(PRIVACY.md and the privacy notes came with 7.2.)*

## 7.5 iPhone widgets · 1 weekend
[UX: iPhone ▸ Widgets](../product/05-ux-iphone.md#widgets). First set: **Pulse, Remember and Rhythm**.
- [x] Widgets in the `FunesIntents` bundle (next to the Control), reading `OverviewCache` through one timeline provider; a midnight entry moves the days along. *(`OverviewProvider` in `Apps/FunesIntents/OverviewTimeline.swift`, the views in `OverviewWidgets.swift`. The entries are FunesPhone's `WidgetSnapshot` (`WidgetSnapshotTests`): the 30-day answer, moved along to each entry's day in the Mac's time zone by `VaultOverview.moved(to:)`, which empties the days since the snapshot, drops the oldest, and works this week's numbers and the agents' shared counts out again from the days. Entries at now, when "as of" begins, when the dimming begins, and the next two midnights; then WidgetKit asks again.)*
- [x] **Pulse** (medium): the week's numbers and the 14-day in/out chart. **Remember** (small): Remember and 🎙 buttons (`OpenCaptureIntent`, typing or dictating). **Rhythm** (small: 6 weeks; medium: 12 weeks and the streak). *(Pulse: "Funes · this week", "7 saved · 7 shared · 1 refused" (refused only when there were any), `PulseChart` without its axis or a selection, and "Shared with Claude Code, Cursor, Codex" under it. Remember: 🎙 is `OpenDictationIntent` (not offered in Shortcuts), which leaves `CaptureLaunch` a `.dictate` request: Home starts its own dictation (iOS 26), or focuses the field before that, as its mic does. Rhythm medium: the grid, then the streak, the busiest weekday and "41 new in 12 weeks", in Home's words. Pulse and Rhythm open Home through their `widgetURL`.)*
- [x] The app reloads timelines after every cache write; the background refresh (`com.funesvault.ios.deliver`) also fetches the overview when the Mac answers. *(`OverviewModel.onCacheChange`, after each write and on clear, and entering or leaving the demo, reloads all of them. The background refresh asks for the 30 days after delivering; a failure keeps the cache, and Not paired or Removed deletes it as in the app.)*
- [x] "as of" after an hour, dimmed after a day; Not paired and Demo states; agent names `.privacySensitive()`. *(The cache file now says whether it's the demo's (`OverviewCache.Contents.isDemo`), and never mixes the two; a demo's left behind by an app closed in the demo is deleted when the app starts on the real pairing. The demo shows **DEMO**, never "as of". Without counts, the Keychain's pairing says "Pair with your Mac" (Remember says it too, and still works) from "Your pulse shows here". The gallery shows the demo's counts. Dynamic Type is capped at xxLarge; the charts, the grid and the Remember button are `widgetAccentable` for the tinted and clear Home Screens; each widget reads as one VoiceOver element with its numbers, Remember as its two buttons.)*
- [ ] Later, not in this step: Memories, the large Funes widget, Pulse large, Lock Screen widgets.
- [x] When the code lands: README, docs, CHANGELOG.

## Exit criteria
- The author uses Home on both devices for a week; the Mac opens on it, and captures made from it reach the Inbox exactly as from the capture box.
- The iPhone shows nothing in its headers while connected; every banner state has its bar, and Demo is always labelled.
- `overview.get` and the cache never carry memory text or titles (tests); Home and the widgets show the cache with "As of" while the Mac is away.
- Pulse, Remember and Rhythm on the author's Home Screen for a week, refreshed without opening the app at least daily when the Mac is reachable.
